Deterministic Verifier

One import type. Zero runtime imports. No model, network, filesystem, clock or randomness. 21 checks across three pillars, integer paise.

✓ VERIFIEDRecomputed state supports the claim under the policy in force when the decision was taken.
? UNCERTAINEvidence incomplete, stale or unenforceable. AVOS does not know, and says so.
✕ FAILEDEvidence refutes the claim, or integrity is broken. Closing would move money wrongly.
THE FOUR PILLARS
GUARD — 6 checksPermissible under the policy in force at decision_time, not today's. Catches anachronistic judgement, freshness breaches, impermissible states.
PROVE — 6 checksEvery row carries source file, row id, content hash and freshness. Re-hashed on replay, so a mutated source is caught rather than silently re-verified.
VERIFY — 9 checksexpected = gross − refunds − calcFees(policy) − holds against the bank credit. Fees from the rate card, never from a recorded fee.
MEASURE — 8 gatesPrecision, false closure, value coverage, exception detection, abstention accuracy — plus a hard slice that is reported, never gated.
Why the boundary is the product. StructuredClaim has three fields. There is no slot an agent's rationale or confidence could occupy, so passing one is a compile error rather than a review comment. evals/isolation.ts asserts on every run that the verifier has zero runtime imports and never names the quarantined free-text field. A prompt injected into a bank memo is therefore not filtered — it is on a data path that does not reach a verdict, and the adversarial suite proves it by asserting the verdict object is byte-identical with the attacker's text present and blanked.
